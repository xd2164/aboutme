Xiaoxue Du

I study how AI can augment human learning — and build tools that make that real. My work spans frontier AI technologies, AI literacy, education strategy, and the infrastructure needed to deploy AI responsibly at scale. I develop curriculum frameworks, agentic tools, and field systems for K–12 and postsecondary education, with a particular focus on learners who have been historically underserved.

I have collaborated with the MIT Media Lab, Stanford HAI, frontier AI labs, and non-profit partners. My tools are used by teachers and students across the US, and my books are published by Corwin Press and Springer Nature.

About

I am a Senior Program Officer at the Gates Foundation's US Programs, where I support AI strategy, infrastructure, and deployment for K–12 and postsecondary education. I work alongside foundation colleagues, university researchers, and AI companies including Anthropic to build the field conditions for responsible, equitable AI adoption in education.

My research centers on AI literacy — what it means to understand AI well enough to question it — and how to design systems that build that capacity rather than bypass it. I have published two books (Corwin 2025, Springer 2026) and peer-reviewed papers with co-authors from MIT Media Lab, Columbia University, and Teachers College. My work spans curriculum frameworks, game-based learning, professional development, and agentic AI safety.

I am also a contributor to the Human Context Protocol (MIT, Stanford, Oxford — Shah et al.), an open architecture for user-governed AI memory and personalization; a modeler of frontier agentic AI safety risks applied to education infrastructure; and an external evaluator for the NSF-funded Science Pathways Scholars Program (SP)² at Barnard College (Award 2030903).

I hold an EdD from Teachers College, Columbia University. Before the Gates Foundation I held positions at the University of Chicago and collaborated with the MIT Media Lab on AI literacy and human-AI interaction research.

Projects

Teacher Co-Pilot live
Design · Create · Reflect · memory-enabled instructional assistant · K–12

A chat-based planning partner that converts learning goals into lesson plans, prompts, and assessments across any K–12 subject — grounded in the Big Five Ideas in AI from the Corwin and Springer books. Not a one-shot generator: three phases — Design (frame goals, surface student questions, connect to standards), Create (build activities and materials through natural conversation), and Reflect (save post-lesson insights as reusable memory). No student AI accounts needed. Export to Markdown in one click.

Teen AI Explorer Kit live
Five Big Ideas in AI · five curriculum kits · total beginners · no coding required

A structured youth starter kit built directly from the Corwin handbook — four zones (Play & Explore, Build Blocks, Code & Create, Project Prompts) and five deep-dive curriculum kits, one per Big Idea. Each kit contains a 5-step build sequence, three reflect prompts, cross-disciplinary lessons with NGSS and CCSS standards, and a core ethical question from the book. Every tool is free; no accounts required. Companion to companion.corwin.com/courses/TeachingAILiteracy.

Research Discovery Kit open source
RAG · LLM evidence grading · decision briefs · observatory interface · MIT license

A synthesis platform combining retrieval-augmented generation with LLM evidence grading to produce structured decision briefs. Human Context Protocol live · hcp.me
memory · consent · cross-system handoff · MIT · Stanford · Oxford (Shah et al.)

Built hcp.me — a personalization demo implementing the Human Context Protocol, a user-centric architecture for secure, interoperable AI preference management. Underlying research by Shah, South, Evans, Kirk, Pei, Trask, Weyl & Bakker (MIT, Stanford, Oxford) proposes a standard for how AI systems should manage, store, and transfer personal context without ceding user control. The demo shows memory layers, granular consent flows, and cross-system handoff working end-to-end.

EDUAgent internal · Gates Foundation
Claude API · field intelligence · evidence synthesis · US Programs portfolio

An AI field intelligence agent powering evidence discovery, market insights, and R&D synthesis for the Foundation's US Programs portfolio.
